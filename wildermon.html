The Wildermon Stud Book

Everything that walks, swims, burrows or comes at you on this island: what it is for, what it is worth in numbers, and the whole pool of blood any one of them can be born with. Read straight off the rules the island runs on.

The roster

Base figures for a grown animal with no traits counted. Blood moves every one of them.

Nothing on the island answers to that.

Blood

Every wildermon of every species is born with three traits, drawn from the one pool. Nothing here is species-bound; a rabba may be born ironsides.

Plain blood

A plain trait is its tier: scrappy is common, ironsides is fantastic. One roll picks both.

Fighting blood

Thirty names, each in all four grades. The roll picks the name, then rolls the grade for it off the same odds; no animal carries two grades of one name.
